Privacy Policy for Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views

Effective Date: August 2026

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views is an offline sliding puzzle game featuring scenic locations, landscapes, and views from across New Zealand. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Data Collection

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal or sensitive user data.

The app does not require account creation, login, or submission of personal information.

Local Data Storage

Game progress, unlocked artwork, avatars, rewards, and user preferences are stored locally on the user's device only. No data is uploaded to external servers or cloud services by the app.

Uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored progress data.

In-App Purchases

The app offers an optional one-time purchase to unlock additional content. All payments are processed securely through Google Play Billing.

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not collect, store, or have access to payment or financial information. Payment processing is handled independently by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

Third-Party Services

The app is distributed through Google Play and uses Google Play Billing to process optional in-app purchases. Google may process certain technical, device, account, and transaction-related information as necessary to provide these services.

This information is handled by Google in accordance with Google's own terms and privacy policies. Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views does not independently collect, store, or access users' payment or financial information.

Sharing Content

The app includes an optional sharing feature that allows users to share completed artwork using other applications installed on their device. This action is initiated by the user and occurs locally on the device. The app does not track, log, or store shared content.

Notifications

The app may offer optional local notifications to remind or encourage users to return to the game. Notifications are only enabled with the user's permission.

Local notifications are scheduled and processed on the user's device. The app does not use a remote notification server or collect personal information for notification purposes. Users may decline notification permission when prompted or disable notifications at any time through their device settings.

Children’s Privacy

Sliding Puzzles: Kiwi Views is intended for a general audience. The app does not require user accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

International Users

The app is distributed globally via Google Play. As no personal data is collected or processed by the developer, user privacy rights under applicable laws, including GDPR, are respected by design.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date above.
